Current flagship experience

On the Water

High school students investigate Puget Sound aboard a scientific vessel through active learning stations focused on nearshore habitats, watershed connections, water quality, field methods, and environmental careers.

  • Scientific vessel experience
  • Water quality and field methods
  • Marine career exploration
  • Stewardship and environmental literacy

On the Shoreline

A companion experience with the same core learning outcomes, delivered through accessible shoreline investigations. Four rotating stations explore underwater observation, water quality, plankton, and human impacts and restoration.

Future pilot dates have not yet been scheduled.

  • Accessible shoreline setting
  • Intertidal and nearshore science
  • Community science and stewardship
  • Environmental justice connections for older grades
Designed to work independently or together. Schools may choose either experience as a standalone program or combine both to compare shoreline and open-water ecosystems while reinforcing scientific practices, stewardship, and marine career pathways.
